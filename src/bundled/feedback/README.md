# Feedback

Send feedback about Buzz to the people who run your community.

## Where you'll find it

- **Send feedback** in the menu under your profile picture.

## If you turn it off

- **Send feedback** disappears from the menu.

## Good to know

- Feedback goes to the feedback inbox of the community you're connected to, not to a channel. It's sent from your account, so it isn't anonymous.
- Images and diagnostics are stored like other media in that community: people allowed to view its media can open them if they have the link.
- An image uploads as soon as you choose it. Removing it or closing the form doesn't delete the upload.
- **Attach diagnostics** adds the time, app version, platform, user agent and language. No app logs are included.
