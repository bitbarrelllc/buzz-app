# Hosted communities

Create and manage communities hosted by Builderlab, using your Builderlab account.

## Where you'll find it

- **Settings → Communities → Hosted communities**. Choose **Sign in with Builderlab** to start.
- From there you can **Create community**, **Copy address**, **Transfer**, **Archive**, **Unarchive** and **Delete**.

## If you turn it off

- The **Hosted communities** card disappears and you're signed out of Builderlab here. Your communities aren't changed, and you stay connected to the ones you've joined.

## Good to know

- Sign-in opens your browser. It's separate from the Builderlab plugin's sign-in and is kept only in memory. You don't need it to join or use communities.
- **Connect Buzz identity** links your Builderlab account to this device's Buzz identity. Buzz signs a one-time challenge on your computer; your private key never leaves it. **Unpair identity** removes the link, and community actions stop until you connect again.
- Creating a community copies its address but doesn't join it. Join with **Add a community** (+) in the community rail.
- Archiving stops all connections, but the community still counts toward your limit.
- **Delete** appears only on archived communities, and only if your account is allowed to delete. Deletion is permanent and can't be canceled, and the address stays reserved for good.
- **Transfer** gives ownership to someone else and makes you a regular member. They need a connected Buzz identity first, and it can't be undone.
