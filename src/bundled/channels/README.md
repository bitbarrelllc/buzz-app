# Channels

Channels is where your conversations happen in Buzz. It gives you the Messages page with your channels, direct messages and threads.

## Where you'll find it

- **Messages** in the top bar, with your channels and **Direct messages** in the sidebar.
- **Create channel** in the sidebar. Choose **Ongoing** or **Temporary**, and optionally start from a **Template**.
- **Channel actions** on an open channel, with options such as **Leave channel** and **Archive channel**.
- Type # in a message to link to a channel.
- **Settings → Messages** to choose where threads open: **Side pane** or **Main pane**.
- **Settings → Channel templates** to save agents, teams and a starting Canvas for new channels.

## If you turn it off

- You can't. Channels is required, so Settings shows **Required** instead of a switch. Buzz needs it to show your messages, and turns it back on if an older install saved it as off.

## Good to know

- Your thread setting and channel templates are saved on this device only. They don't follow you to other devices.
- A **Temporary** channel cleans up after a period without activity. An **Ongoing** channel keeps its history until you archive it.
- Archiving a channel archives it for everyone. Its messages are kept, and it can be unarchived later.
