# Inbox

Inbox gathers your mentions, direct messages, and replies in threads you're part of, so you can catch up in one place.

## Where you'll find it

- **Inbox** in the sidebar. Use the **Inbox filters** menu to show **Threads**, **Mentions**, or **DMs**, messages from **People** or **Agents**, or your **Archived** conversations. The bell button shows unread only.
- Select a conversation to read it and reply without leaving Inbox. Right-click it for **Archive conversation**, **Restore conversation**, or **Mark unread**.
- **Drafts**, in the filters menu, lists messages you started but haven't sent.

## If you turn it off

- The Inbox page and its Drafts list go away.
- Unread counts and message notifications keep working, and your drafts stay in the conversations where you wrote them.

## Good to know

- Archiving is just for you and is saved only on this device. It doesn't sync to your other devices.
- An archived conversation comes back to your Inbox when something new arrives in it, like a new mention or reply.
- Drafts are also saved only on this device.
