# Me

Me is your personal workspace for chatting with your agents.

## Where you'll find it

- **Me** in the top bar. Choose **New chat** to start one, or press Cmd+N (Ctrl+N on Windows and Linux).
- Open a chat's menu to **Rename** it, **Move to** a project, or **Share…** it. Projects in Me are your own folders for chats, separate from the Projects plugin.

## If you turn it off

- Your Me chats appear in the **Messages** sidebar instead. Nothing is deleted.
- The new chat shortcut stops working.

## Good to know

- A Me chat is a regular private channel with you and the agents you add. Keeping it in Me changes only where it's listed, not who can read it.
- **Share…** moves the chat into Messages with its full history, Canvas, and agents. If you turn off **Private** while sharing, everyone in the community can read its full history.
- Me chats can use only your available agents.
