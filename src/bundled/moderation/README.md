# Community admin

Lets community owners and admins invite people and manage who's in the community.

## Where you'll find it

- **Settings → Administration → Membership**, shown only to owners and admins of the selected community.
- **Invite members** to add someone by their public key, or to copy an invite link with an expiry and an optional use limit.
- Each member's actions menu: **Make admin**, **Make member** or **Remove**.

## If you turn it off

- **Membership** disappears from Settings, and Buzz has no other place to invite people or manage community members.

## Good to know

- Admins can invite people and remove members. Only owners can use **Make admin** and **Make member**, pick a role when adding someone, or remove an admin. Nobody can change or remove the owner, or themselves.
- Anyone with an invite link can use it to join until it expires or runs out of uses.
- Removed members lose access until they're invited again.
