# Voice Notes

Lets you record voice notes in the composer and play them in conversations.

## Where you'll find it

- **Record voice note** (the microphone) in the composer. Use **Finish voice note** to attach it or **Discard voice note** to throw it away. Recording finishes by itself at 5 minutes.
- Voice notes in messages play in a player with a waveform, seeking and playback speed.

## If you turn it off

- **Record voice note** goes away.
- Voice notes you receive still play, in the standard audio player.

## Good to know

- Recording needs microphone access for Buzz.
- A finished recording starts uploading to your community as soon as it's attached, before you press Send. Removing it from the draft stops an upload in progress but doesn't delete one that already finished.
- An unsent recording is kept only while Buzz is open. Reloading or restarting loses it.
